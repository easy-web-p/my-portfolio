## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the concept of "Playful Intelligence"—a synthesis of rigorous computational precision and spontaneous creative experimentation. The design language speaks to design-forward technologists, creative directors, and product visionaries who demand elite execution without sterile corporate monotony.

The aesthetic fuses modern editorial layout discipline with tactile, reactive micro-affordances:
- **Architectural Foundation**: Deep, ink-like contrast, wide rhythmic margins, and crisp geometric structures establish instant credibility and authority.
- **Electric Accents & Micro-Surprises**: Kinetic nodes, high-voltage violet pulses, bright lime callouts, and modular sticker badges punctuate an otherwise disciplined canvas.
- **Tone & Mood**: Cerebral yet buoyant, sharp yet warm, sophisticated without self-importance. Interactions feel responsive, springy, and deliberate.

## Colors

The palette operates under a high-contrast editorial hierarchy, punctuated by high-chroma beacons:

- **Primary (`#8B5CF6` - Electric Violet)**: The core luminous beacon used for high-impact interactive anchors, focal links, active ring strokes, and dynamic kinetic states.
- **Secondary (`#111827` - Deep Midnight Navy)**: Grounding structural tone for primary headers, high-emphasis framing, dark buttons, and heavy structural dividers.
- **Tertiary (`#B8F34A` - Hyper Lime)**: High-visibility kinetic pop used sparingly for pill status tags, playful badge fills, high-friction hover offsets, and key quantitative callouts.
- **Background (`#FAF9F6` - Warm Gallery White)**: An inviting, paper-like off-white canvas that softens screen fatigue and distinguishes the interface from clinical default whites.
- **Neutral / Text (`#1F2937` - Deep Charcoal)**: The primary reading shade for body copy and nuanced text elements, offering crisp contrast without the harshness of pure black.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Clean elevated card fills providing separation above the canvas background.

### Tint & Opacity Rules
- Violet tint washes (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.08)`) serve as active list tile backgrounds and interactive hover fills.
- Lime glow washes (`rgba(184, 243, 74, 0.25)`) emphasize active project flags and dynamic metric badges.

## Typography

Typography balances architectural geometry with conversational warmth:

- **Headlines (Sora)**: Sora provides a progressive, wide-stance geometric structure with distinctive circular apexes and open counters. Used for key impact statements, case study titles, and punchy category markers. Always set tight with slight negative letter tracking on sizes 28px and above.
- **Body & Continuous Reading (Manrope)**: Manrope offers pristine legibility, warm open terminals, and clean horizontal rhythm. It balances Sora's overt geometry by providing a smooth, effortless reading texture across extended project walkthroughs and technical specifications.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy (Sora Uppercase / Semibold)**: Employed for technical badges, index numbers, pill labels, and metadata. Generous tracking on small labels (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) enhances clarity and imparts an engineered, editorial character.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture leverages a structured 12-column editorial grid paired with rhythmic background guidelines:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, 32px gutters, and dynamic margins capping at 1360px content bounds. Wide whitespace margins (`4.5rem` to `6rem` vertical padding) isolate showcase pieces and technical showcases.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, 24px gutters, and 32px safe margins. Secondary modular sidebars stack below hero sections.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px)**: 4 columns, 16px gutters, and 20px page margins. Complex horizontal interactive groups collapse into swipeable cards or single-column stacks.

### Structural Grid Details
Background surfaces optionally incorporate a faint blueprint dot grid (`radial-gradient(#111827 1px, transparent 1px)` with 32px spacing at 4% opacity) to evoke creative drafting precision. Structural vertical borders define editorial column splits.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons diffuse, generic blur drop-shadows in favor of crisp architectural edges and mechanical offset planes:

- **Flat Precision Foundation**: Base surfaces sit flat against the canvas framed by subtle, crisp borders (`1px solid #E5E7EB`).
- **Interactive Lift (Crisp Hard Shadows)**: Active or elevated elements project a solid-edge hard shadow: `4px 4px 0px #111827`. This hard-cast plane creates a tangible, poster-like depth that reinforces tactile feedback.
- **Hover Kinetic Compression**: When hovered or pressed, interactive components shift along the axis (`translate(-2px, -2px)` with shadow expanding to `6px 6px 0px #111827`), providing instantaneous mechanical response.
- **Node & Sticker Layers**: Overlapping floating badges use a tiny sharp shadow (`2px 2px 0px #111827`) with micro rotations (-1.5° to 2°) to break standard vertical alignment with deliberate playfulness.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a fluid contrast between generous, friendly card enclosures and tight pill geometry:

- **Rounded Enclosures**: Containers, interactive showcase modules, and media wrappers carry a roundedness token of `2` (yielding base `0.5rem` [8px], with prominent case-study card panels scaling up to `1.25rem`–`1.75rem` [20px–28px] matching the spec).
- **Pills & Badges**: Interactive chips, status flags, and micro-tags utilize full pill encapsulation (`9999px`) to contrast cleanly against large geometric cards.
- **Playful Nodes**: Connecting endpoints and decoration markers use solid circles (8px–12px) paired with thin rule lines (`1.5px`) to visualize algorithmic links and modular component workflows.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Deep Midnight Navy background (`#111827`), pure white Sora label, 10px 24px padding, rounded-lg (`1rem`), 1.5px solid `#111827`. On hover, translates `translate(-2px, -2px)` casting a `4px 4px 0px #8B5CF6` shadow.
- **Electric Accent Button**: Electric Violet fill (`#8B5CF6`), white bold text, transforms on hover with hard-edge shadow `4px 4px 0px #B8F34A`.
- **Ghost / Outlined**: Transparent surface, 1.5px border in `#111827`, charcoal text. On hover: fills with light violet wash (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.08)`).

### Badges & Sticker Pills
- **Lime Metric Badge**: Hyper Lime fill (`#B8F34A`), `#111827` bold label text, 4px 12px padding, full pill radius, framed by a delicate 1px dark stroke. Rotates slightly (-2°) when nested over image corners.
- **Status Node Badge**: Translucent violet background, `#8B5CF6` text, featuring a glowing 6px pulsing node indicator dot at the left edge.

### Cards & Showcase Panels
- Built with crisp white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`), `1.25rem` to `1.75rem` (20px-28px) corner curvature, and a defining perimeter line (`1px solid #E5E7EB`).
- Top-right corner features an interactive floating arrow node that snaps outward on card hover.
- Cards react to hover states by projecting the signature sharp-edge shadow (`6px 6px 0px #111827`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Warm white or pure white backgrounds, clean 1.5px `#E5E7EB` border, Manrope 15px text, rounded-md (`0.5rem`). Focusing triggers a crisp `#8B5CF6` border paired with an unblurred offset ring `0 0 0 2px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.2)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom square/round shapes with a distinct 1.5px `#111827` border; active states fill solid `#8B5CF6` with a crisp white geometric check mark.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- Horizontal editorial divider rows (`1px solid #E5E7EB`) with generous 20px vertical breathing room.
- Hovering over a row highlights the background with a soft warm violet tint, reveals an arrow glyph, and displays contextual metadata tags.
